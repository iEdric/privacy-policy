Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 28, 2026 · Applies to PoseHub for iOS, version 1.0

PoseHub helps you find photo inspiration, follow pose references and edit your photos. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use these features.

Your photos stay under your control. No PoseHub account is required. Camera frames, imported reference photos and pose keypoints are processed on your device. PoseHub does not upload them to developer servers or use them to train AI models.

1. Information handled on your device

We do not perform facial identification, create biometric identity profiles, or score attractiveness, body shape or health. We do not use your camera frames or photos for AI model training. Built-in reference images are AI-generated illustration material, not photos submitted by users.

2. Permissions and your choices

  • Camera: Requested when you enable photography, for preview, capture and local pose guidance.
  • Photo selection: The system photo picker gives access to the reference photo you choose, without requiring full photo-library access.
  • Adding photos: Requested when you export a photo to Apple Photos. The app does not request full-library reading permission for export.

You can deny or change permissions in iOS Settings. The reference library remains available without camera permission. If exporting is denied, you can still keep the photo in the app. Spoken guidance uses speech synthesis; the app does not request microphone access or record your voice.

3. Purchases

Optional PoseHub Pro is a non-consumable, one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. StoreKit provides product and verified transaction information so the app can determine access and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment card information or Apple Account password. Purchase processing, refunds and Apple-held transaction records follow Apple’s privacy policy and applicable Apple terms.

Deleting local app data does not cancel a completed non-consumable purchase or remove Apple’s transaction records. Restore purchases using the same Apple Account.

4. Sharing, email feedback and local drafts

Photos leave the app when you explicitly share them through the iOS share sheet. You choose the destination app or service. PoseHub has no automatic feedback upload or developer-run photo upload endpoint.

Feedback is saved as a local draft. PoseHub can prepare an email addressed to our support mailbox, openchatcl@outlook.com. You review and confirm sending in Mail or your selected email app. The recipient receives the message, your sender email address and any information you choose to include. You can optionally attach the app version, iOS version and interface language; this is off by default. The app does not automatically attach photos, device identifiers or transaction records.

Opening another mail app does not confirm delivery. When Apple’s mail composer reports that a message is queued, the app shows that handoff status; Mail is responsible for sending it. You can also copy the recipient address and message and send them through your usual email service. Copying is not an automatic submission; clipboard handling and system synchronization follow iOS settings.

The receiving email or sharing service handles content under its privacy practices. Information you voluntarily send to the developer is used to respond to your request. Avoid portrait photos, payment details or other sensitive information unless needed to explain the issue.

5. No advertising or tracking

The current app has no advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics SDK or developer-operated analytics backend. It does not request advertising-tracking permission. We do not sell or rent your photos or other personal information. Apple system services, device diagnostics and backup settings are managed separately by Apple and your iOS preferences.

6. Retention, deletion and backups

Local feedback drafts remain until you clear them. The app clears its draft when Apple’s mail composer reports that a message is queued. Cancelling, saving to Mail drafts, a sending error or opening another email app keeps the app draft. Messages already saved or sent in a mail service must be managed separately there.

Temporary camera frames, body keypoints and imported reference images are used during the active session rather than retained as a personal record. Photos you save in the app remain until you delete them or remove the app. Edited versions may be separate copies, and deleting one version does not remove other saved versions. Remove favorites by toggling the heart.

Delete a saved photo from its preview in the local gallery. Delete exported or shared copies separately in Apple Photos or the receiving service. Uninstalling the app removes its local app container. The app does not operate a cloud gallery, but iOS device backups and Apple Photos/iCloud synchronization may include saved data depending on your settings; manage those copies with Apple. No PoseHub account exists to delete.

7. Security and children

The app uses iOS app sandboxing and the device’s local file-storage protections. Keep your device and operating system secure and up to date. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

PoseHub is a general photography tool and is not specifically directed at children. It has no user-registration form or public photo-sharing community and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through a developer-run service. A parent or guardian can use the privacy contact below for concerns about information they have voluntarily sent through a support channel.

8. This policy page

Inside PoseHub, this policy is loaded from the app bundle without fetching a developer-hosted webpage. In-app viewing uses a temporary website data store, does not load third-party resources, and opens user-selected external links in the system browser.

This static HTML page contains no analytics code, advertising scripts, third-party fonts or embedded tracking resources. If you open an external link, the destination website’s privacy practices apply. The website hosting this policy may handle ordinary request and server logs according to its own deployment settings; those are separate from photos processed locally in the app.

9. Changes and privacy contact

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. New permissions or features will be explained before use where required.

The developer is identified on the PoseHub App Store product page. For feedback, questions about this policy, or requests concerning information you voluntarily provided to support, email openchatcl@outlook.com. You can also use the developer contact available through App Support on that product page. The same full policy is available offline in the app under My space → About & privacy.

隐私政策

生效及更新日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日 · 适用于 PoseHub iOS 版 1.0

PoseHub 帮助你寻找摄影灵感、跟随姿势参考并编辑照片。本政策说明你使用这些功能时，相关信息会如何处理。

照片由你掌控。PoseHub 无需注册账户。相机画面、你导入的参考照片及人体关键点均在设备上处理。PoseHub 不会将这些内容上传到开发者服务器，也不会用它们训练 AI 模型。

一、设备上处理的信息

信息用途保存方式

我们不进行人脸身份识别，不建立生物识别身份档案，也不对颜值、身材或健康状况评分。你的相机画面和照片不会用于 AI 模型训练。内置姿势图是 AI 生成的参考素材，并非用户上传的照片。

二、权限与你的选择

  • 相机：在你开启拍摄时申请，用于预览、拍照及本地姿势指导。
  • 选择参考照片：通过系统照片选择器，仅访问你主动选择的内容，无需完整相册读取权限。
  • 添加照片：在你导出到系统相册时申请，仅用于添加照片，不因此申请完整相册读取权限。

你可以拒绝授权，也可以在 iOS 系统设置中修改权限。不授权相机仍可浏览姿势参考；拒绝导出权限时仍可将照片保存在 App 内。朗读建议使用系统语音合成，App 不申请麦克风权限，也不录制你的声音。

三、内购

PoseHub Pro 是可选的一次性非消耗型内购，由 Apple 处理。App 通过 StoreKit 提供的商品和已验证交易信息判断及恢复权益。开发者不接收你的银行卡信息或 Apple 账户密码。支付、退款及 Apple 保存的交易记录适用 Apple 隐私政策和相关条款。

删除 App 本地资料不会取消已完成的非消耗型购买，也不会删除 Apple 的交易记录。你可以使用相同 Apple 账户恢复购买。

四、分享、邮件反馈与本地草稿

只有当你主动通过 iOS 系统分享面板分享照片时，内容才会交给你选择的 App 或服务。PoseHub 不会自动上传反馈，也不提供开发者运营的照片上传接口。

反馈会先保存为本地草稿。你可以编写发给支持邮箱 openchatcl@outlook.com 的邮件，并在 Mail 或你选择的邮件应用中审核、确认发送。接收方会获得正文、发件邮箱及你主动提供的信息。你可以选择附上 App 版本、iOS 版本和界面语言，默认关闭；App 不会自动附带照片、设备标识或交易记录。

打开其他邮件应用不代表已送达。Apple 邮件编辑器报告邮件已排入队列时，App 仅说明已交由 Mail 处理，实际发送由 Mail 负责。你也可以复制联系邮箱和完整正文，通过常用邮箱自行发送；复制不是自动提交，剪贴板处理及系统同步遵循 iOS 设置。

接收邮件或分享内容的服务适用其自身隐私规则。你主动向开发者发送的信息用于答复相关请求。除非解释问题所必需，请勿附带人像、支付信息或其他敏感内容。

五、广告与追踪

当前 App 不包含广告、跨 App 追踪、第三方统计 SDK 或开发者运营的分析后台，不申请广告追踪权限。我们不会出售或出租你的照片及其他个人信息。Apple 系统服务、设备诊断和备份由 Apple 及你的系统设置独立管理。

六、保存、删除与备份

本地反馈草稿会保留至你清除。Apple 邮件编辑器报告邮件已排入发送队列后，App 会清理本机草稿；取消、保存到 Mail 草稿、发送失败或仅打开其他邮件应用，都会保留 App 内草稿。邮件服务中已保存或已发送的消息需在相应服务中单独管理。

相机帧、人体关键点及导入参考图用于当前会话，不作为个人记录长期保存。保存在 App 内的照片会保留至你主动删除或卸载 App。编辑结果可能另存为副本，删除一个版本不会自动删除其他版本；收藏可通过再次点击爱心取消。

你可以在本地图库打开照片预览并删除。导出或分享的副本，需要在系统照片 App 或接收服务中单独删除。卸载 App 会移除本地应用空间。PoseHub 不运营云端图库，但 iOS 设备备份以及系统相册/iCloud 同步可能按你的设置包含已保存内容，需通过 Apple 的相应功能管理。PoseHub 无账户，因此无需进行应用账户注销。

七、安全与未成年人

App 使用 iOS 沙盒和设备本地文件保护机制。请妥善保护设备，并及时更新系统。任何安全措施均无法保证绝对安全。

PoseHub 是通用摄影工具，并非专门面向儿童。App 不提供用户注册或公开照片社区，也不会通过开发者运营的服务主动收集儿童个人信息。对于经支持渠道主动发送的信息，家长或监护人可以通过下方联系方式提出隐私相关问题。

八、本政策网页

在 PoseHub 内，本政策从应用内置文件读取，不请求开发者托管的网页。App 内阅读使用临时网页数据存储，不加载第三方资源；你主动打开的外部链接交由系统浏览器处理。

本静态 HTML 不含统计代码、广告脚本、第三方字体或嵌入式追踪资源。打开外部链接后，适用目标网站的隐私规则。托管本页面的网站可能按其实际部署设置处理常规访问和服务器日志；这与 App 在设备上处理的照片数据相互独立。

九、政策更新与联系

如信息处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策及日期，并在需要时于使用前说明相关功能或权限。

开发者身份以 PoseHub 的 App Store 产品页为准。如有意见反馈、隐私政策问题，或需处理你主动向支持渠道提供的信息，请发送邮件至 openchatcl@outlook.com，也可通过产品页的“App 支持”所列联系方式联系开发者。App 内“我的 → 关于与隐私”离线提供同一份完整隐私政策。
